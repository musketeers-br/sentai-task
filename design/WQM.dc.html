AlwaysQueue
With AlwaysQueue on, work always goes through the WQM queue, even when a worker is free and the process could run it directly.
On, every step appears in the history with distinct TimeQueued and TimeStarted — that is what keeps the run timeline auditable. Off, short steps disappear from the queue accounting.
Changing this category affects 7 scheduled tasks beyond this wave. The change takes effect on the next dispatch — runs in flight keep the ceilings they started with.
