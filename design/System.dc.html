DARK THEME — DEFAULT FOR THE NIGHT WINDOW

#141A26 card
#2B3648 border

Ink-blue ground with low overall luminance, cards lighter than the ground, and no pure black surface — pure black glares against text at 3 a.m.
LIGHT THEME — DESIGNED SEPARATELY
#EFEDE7 ground
#FFFFFF card
#C9C3B6 border
#16181D text
Warm paper, white card: the hierarchy inverts against the dark theme — there the card rises, here it lightens against paper. A 1 px shadow replaces the selection halo.

running pointing triangle

completed filled circle with a check
failed filled warning triangle
cancelled slashed circle
No state colour is reused as a UI accent. In monochrome or under colour blindness, the six shapes stay distinct.
